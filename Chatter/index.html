About Chatter

Chatter is a multi-functional platform where authors and readers can come together to share their content. It is a text-based platform where users can create and share content, engage with other users, and discover new content. Chatter is a platform that encourages creativity and collaboration, providing a space for writers and readers to connect and share their passion for the written word.

Our goal is to create a community of individuals who are passionate about writing and reading. We believe that everyone has a story to tell, and Chatter is the perfect place to share that story.

Why you should join Chatter

Our goal is to make writers and readers live on one platform so that they can interact, engage and share their content easily. We aim to create a community of writers and readers who can connect and share their passion for the written word.

Analytics

Analytics to track the number of views, likes and comments on your content and also the performance of your content over a period of time.

Social interactions

Users on the platform can interact with other users by liking, commenting and also following other users.

Content creation

Write very easily, write on the go with our easy to use editor and also schedule your content to be published at a later date.
